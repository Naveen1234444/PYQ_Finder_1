Question record format

Each record should preserve the original wording and source. Fields: year, paper, question_number, subject, topic, subtopic, question, options (A–D), answer, explanation, source, tags.

[
 {"year":2015,"paper":"GS Paper I","question_number":1,
  "subject":"Polity","topic":"Fundamental Rights",
  "subtopic":"Right to Equality",
  "question":"Original question text",
  "options":{"A":"Option one","B":"Option two","C":"Option three","D":"Option four"},
  "answer":"B","explanation":"","source":"UPSC CSE Prelims 2015"}
]
PDF extraction can recover selectable text. Scanned pages need OCR and manual verification. Extraction does not automatically create verified question/answer records; match the official answer key separately.
